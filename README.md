This repository contains writing samples

1. Child Marriage, Legal Regimes, and Life Outcomes (Master's Thesis)
Title page and abstract. Full thesis available on request. Combines a theoretical model of marriage market equilibrium with a spatial regression discontinuity design across eleven border pairs in sub-Saharan Africa to study how legal enforcement of minimum marriage ages shapes women's education, fertility, and labour market outcomes.
2. Does Access to Loans Lead to Differences in Capital Market Investment Decisions Depending on Financial Standing? (Behavioural Economics Term Paper)
Title page and introduction. Proposes a lab-in-the-field experiment on Amazon Mechanical Turk examining whether loan access changes investment decisions differently for poorer and richer participants, drawing on Shah et al.'s scarcity framework.
